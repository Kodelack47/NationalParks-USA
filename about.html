About The Eco-Life

Connecting people with nature, responsibly.

The Eco-Life is a travel site that promotes sustainable, eco-friendly outdoor experiences. It connects travelers with nature while encouraging the conservation that keeps these places wild.

What you can do here

  • Find a national park: search more than 350 U.S. parks, monuments, and historic sites by state, type, or keyword, with addresses, phone numbers, official websites, and maps.
  • Explore the mountains: a guide to New Hampshire's 48 peaks over 4,000 feet, with elevation, difficulty, and a description of each.
  • Book a tour: request a guided hike on the date that works for you.

Leave no trace

Plan ahead, stay on marked trails, pack out everything you bring in, respect wildlife, and leave what you find. Small habits keep parks beautiful for the next visitor.

About this project

The Eco-Life was built by Ousmana Tunkara as a front-end web development capstone, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Park information is based on U.S. National Park Service listings.
